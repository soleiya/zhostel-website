Z Street Halloween Party in Poblacion, Makati is back!

2022-10-26

Attention Poblacion regulars! Save the date as the Z Hostel group, in collaboration with the team behind Kampai, is geared up once again with their awesome street party events! On October 31, fun will fill up the streets of this beloved neighborhood for “Ready Poblacion One: A Z Street Halloween Special powered by Budweiser.” The event will feature live bands and DJs, food booths, a Parade of Costume contest, and many more surprises!

For video game players, it’s time to put down your phones for a night and take the excitement to the streets! Bring your entire crew and come in your video game character or avatar-inspired costumes and get a chance to win special prizes. Here are the mechanics:

Participating venues include Z Hostel, Green Door, The Apartment, Kampai, Ebi 10, Buccaneers, Alamat, Japonesa, Keepers, Only Pans, Mahalo, and the partial stretch of Don Pedro and Alfonso Streets in Poblacion, Makati.

Since 2017, the Z Hostel group has organized events such as St. Patrick’s Day, Cinco De Mayo, RePoblacion: Philippine Independence Day Party, and Halloween Street Party in Poblacion, gathering up to 25,000 attendees in total and in partnership with some of the biggest brands in the entertainment and food & beverage industries.

We’ve all missed the fun for two years so get ready to go all out this Halloween!

The organizers are extending their special thanks to Mayor Abby Binay and the City of Makati for their support, and to our Platinum sponsor: Budweiser “Halloween Is Yours To Take”. Thanks to our other sponsors Jameson Irish Whiskey, Winston, The Orange Platform, When In Manila, Only Pans, Angkas and Magic 89.9.

Stay tuned for more event updates at Z Hostel Philippines Facebook page and @zhostelph and @kampaiph on Instagram.
