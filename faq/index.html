Z Hostel FAQ

Everything you need to know about staying at Z Hostel, the original Poblacion hostel in Makati, Manila.

Where is Z Hostel?

Z Hostel is at 5660 Don Pedro St. Brgy. Poblacion, Makati City, Philippines 1210 — in the middle of Poblacion, Makati's famous food and nightlife neighborhood in Metro Manila. Bars, restaurants, cafes and clubs are a short walk away, P. Burgos Street is walking distance, and Power Plant Mall (Rockwell) and Century City Mall are less than a kilometer away.

Why is Z Hostel called the original Poblacion hostel?

Z Hostel was here before Poblacion became Manila's best-known nightlife district. It brought travelers from all over the world to what was then a quiet corner of Makati, and the neighborhood's bar and food scene grew up around it. Z is still the social center of Poblacion: the starting point of the daily walking tour and home of the Z Roofdeck.

What kind of rooms does Z Hostel have, and how much do they cost?

Mixed dorms with 4, 6 or 8 beds (from ₱1,250 per bed per night), the all-female 6-bed “Stiletto” dorm (from ₱1,300), Social Fun Floor dorms and private rooms right below the roof deck, and private rooms for two with an en-suite bathroom (from ₱3,000 per room per night). Prices vary by date; you see the exact price for your dates on the booking page.

Is breakfast included?

Yes. Every dorm bed comes with breakfast, and private rooms include breakfast for two, served at the Z Cafe.

How do I book, and is booking direct cheaper?

Book on our own booking page: pick your dates, choose your beds or room, and pay online. You see live availability, get the best rate with no booking-site fees, and your stay is confirmed as soon as your payment goes through.

What are the check-in and check-out times?

Check-in is from 2:00 PM and check-out is until 12:00 noon. Arriving late or early? Let us know when you book and our front desk will help.

What is the Z Roofdeck?

The Z Roofdeck is Z Hostel's world-renowned rooftop bar with a view of the Manila skyline: cold beers, cocktails, food and a DJ deck that hosts local and international DJs and live artists. Every day around 6 PM there's a Sunset Session where in-house guests get one free cocktail. On weekends it turns into a rooftop party.

Is Z Hostel a party hostel? Can I sleep?

Z is a social hostel with a lively roof deck, but most rooms are quiet. If you want to be close to the action, pick the Social Fun Floor right below the roof deck (the bar runs until 3 AM, so you'll hear music there). For a quieter night, choose a regular dorm or a private room on the upper floors.

Is there a female-only dorm?

Yes. The 6-bed female dorm (“Stiletto” room) is for female guests only, with a dresser, vanity mirror and extra-large lockers.

What amenities does Z Hostel have?

Free Wi-Fi, air-conditioning, big custom bunk beds each with a power socket and reading lamp, lockers with power outlets, hot and cold showers, toiletries, a working space, a PC in the lobby, luggage storage, concierge, a front desk, and 24-hour security with CCTV.

How do I get to Z Hostel from the airport (NAIA)?

The easiest way is Grab or a taxi, about ₱250–350 depending on traffic. Ask the driver to take EDSA northbound, exit to Rockwell, and drive through Rockwell to Kalayaan Avenue. Turn right at the Ministop on Kalayaan — that's Don Pedro Street — and Z Hostel is on your left, a few meters from the corner.

Do you accept group bookings?

Yes. For groups, email sales@zhostelph.com or call us and we'll put together the best rooms and rate.

What is there to do around Z Hostel?

Join the free daily walking tour of Poblacion (around 4:30 PM from the lobby), the Sunset Session on the roof deck, and the restaurants of Z Street right next to the hostel. Poblacion is full of hole-in-the-wall restaurants, coffee shops, art spaces, bars and street food, and Rockwell and the Makati CBD are minutes away.
