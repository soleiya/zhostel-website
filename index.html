The OG of Poblacion

Where Poblacion started

Z Hostel is the original Poblacion hostel — the social heart of Makati's most famous neighborhood, on Don Pedro Street in Barangay Poblacion. Since 2015 — long before every corner here had a cocktail bar — travelers and locals have been meeting on the Z Roofdeck. Poblacion grew up around us, and we're still at the center of it.

Stay in a comfy dorm bed or a private room, eat at the Z Cafe, catch the daily Sunset Session on the world-renowned Z Roofdeck, then walk out the door into the bars, restaurants and street food of Poblacion. Breakfast is included with every bed and room.

In 2017 we took the party onto the street: Z Street, our Halloween, Rev Up, Repoblacion and Summer Kick Off street parties with PLUD, has brought more than 300,000 people to Poblacion.

Good to know

Where is Z Hostel?

Z Hostel is at 5660 Don Pedro St. Brgy. Poblacion, Makati City, Philippines 1210 — in the middle of Poblacion, Makati's famous food and nightlife neighborhood in Metro Manila. Bars, restaurants, cafes and clubs are a short walk away, P. Burgos Street is walking distance, and Power Plant Mall (Rockwell) and Century City Mall are less than a kilometer away.

Why is Z Hostel called the original Poblacion hostel?

Z Hostel opened on Don Pedro Street in 2015, before Poblacion became Manila's best-known nightlife district. It brought travelers from all over the world to what was then a quiet corner of Makati, and in 2017 it started Z Street, the street parties that turned Poblacion into Makati's nightlife capital — more than 300,000 people across 25+ parties since. Z is still the social center of Poblacion: the starting point of the daily walking tour and home of the Z Roofdeck.

What kind of rooms does Z Hostel have, and how much do they cost?

Mixed dorms with 4, 6 or 8 beds (from ₱1,250 per bed per night), the all-female 6-bed “Stiletto” dorm (from ₱1,300), Social Fun Floor dorms and private rooms right below the roof deck, and private rooms for two with an en-suite bathroom (from ₱3,000 per room per night). Prices vary by date; you see the exact price for your dates on the booking page.

What is Z Street?

Z Street is the series of street parties Z Hostel started in Poblacion in 2017, co-organized with Poblacion Land & Urban Development Corp. (PLUD): Summer Kick Off (March), Rev Up (May), Repoblacion (June) and Halloween (October, up to 25,000 people; 2026's was Tabi Tabi Po), plus Z Hostel's own New Year's Eve party. More than 300,000 people have joined 25+ Z Street parties. More about Z Street.

When is the Poblacion Halloween street party?

Every year around October 30–31, Z Hostel's Halloween street party takes over Don Pedro, Alfonso and Fermina Streets in Poblacion, right outside the hostel. It's Manila's biggest Halloween street party and October is our busiest month, so book your bed early.
