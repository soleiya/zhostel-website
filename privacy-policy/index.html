Privacy Policy

Flash Packer Inc. (Z Hostel), 5660 Don Pedro St., Poblacion, Makati City, respects your privacy in line with the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173).

What we collect

When you book, pay or contact us we collect your name, email address, mobile number, country, stay dates, the guests in your party, any ID you upload for a Senior Citizen or PWD discount, and your payment records. Online payments are processed by Xendit; we never see or store your card details.

Why we use it

To confirm and manage your reservation, take payment, send you booking emails, meet tax and legal duties, and — only if you have stayed with us — send you occasional offers, which you can unsubscribe from at any time.

Who sees it

Our reservations and front-desk team, our property-management system (Cloudbeds), our payment processor (Xendit) and our email provider. We do not sell your data.

How long we keep it

Booking and payment records are kept as long as the law requires for accounting and tax. Contact-form messages are kept for 12 months.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data at any time by emailing reservations@zhostel.com.

Our website counts visits without cookies; it does not use advertising trackers.
