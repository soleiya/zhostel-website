The OG of Poblacion

The original Poblacion hostel

Ask anyone who has been around Manila's nightlife long enough where the Poblacion scene began, and Z Hostel comes up. When we opened on Don Pedro Street in 2015, Poblacion was a quiet, old corner of Makati of small houses, sari-sari stores and a handful of hole-in-the-wall bars. We built a hostel with a higher standard of beds, a real cafe and a roof deck where travelers and locals could meet — and the neighborhood came alive around it.

Today Poblacion is Manila's best-known food and nightlife district, and Z Hostel is still its center: the place backpackers, digital nomads and Manila locals meet, the starting point of the daily walking tour, and home of the legendary Z Roofdeck.

About Z

Z Hostel is part of a new breed of hostels that combines a higher standard of accommodation with a fun, social atmosphere. Every detail that you find in each room was well thought of to best suit the guest. The Café gives you a taste of authentic Filipino cuisine, while the Z Roof Deck Bar offers great entertainment nightly by live artists and DJs, both local and international. It makes one wonder, how did this all come together?

The managing owners at Z Hostel are a passionate group of friends who took their time to conceptualize and build the hostel from an inspiration. This inspiration began from their Sunday Slowdown sessions at a beach resort called La Luz. There was a certain vibe that was born from music, being around like minded individuals, strangers becoming lifelong friends, and having sophisticated conversations. This was where the foundation of Z Hostel was created.

And it is this same foundation that kept Z going and open throughout the challenging times of the pandemic. We’re going back to full-swing — stronger than ever and with the same brand of good vibes and top-notch service.

What is RFID?

Radio frequency identification, or RFID, is a generic term for technologies that use radio waves to automatically identify people or objects. The most common type used is to store a serial number that identifies a person or object, and perhaps other information on a microchip that is attached to an antenna (the chip and the antenna together are called an RFID transponder or an RFID tag). The antenna enables the chip to transmit the identification information to a reader. The reader converts the radio waves reflected back from the RFID tag into digital information that can then be passed on to computers that can make use of it.

Why use RFID at Z?

This will facilitate cashless transactions. Transactions at the bar will be faster and there will be no need to wait for change because the exact amount will be debited. Efficiency.
